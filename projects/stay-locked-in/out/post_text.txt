TITLE
The Distraction Trap: Same 24 Hours, Two Completely Different Lives

DESCRIPTION
The world doesn't want you to fail. It wants something worse: it wants you distracted.

Two people wake up at 7 AM with the same 24 hours and the same dreams. One grabs his phone. The other gets to work. Ten years later, the gap between them is enormous, and it has nothing to do with talent or luck.

This video is about taking your attention back: start small, put the phone down, and build something you'll be proud of five years from now.

Next time you pick up your phone, ask yourself one question: am I using this device, or is this device using me?
