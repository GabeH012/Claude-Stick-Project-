In 1999, a tiny toy company had one desk, one phone, and thirty days of money left.
Its founder, Maya, had a crazy idea. Instead of selling toys in big stores, she would sell them online.
Everyone laughed. The giant toy chains had billions of dollars and a store in every city.
But Maya kept going.
Ten years later, the giants were closing their stores.
And Maya's little company? It was worth more than all of them combined.
So what did she see that everyone else missed?
