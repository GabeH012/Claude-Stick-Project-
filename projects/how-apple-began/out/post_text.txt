TITLE
How Apple Began: Two Friends, One Garage, and a Small Idea

DESCRIPTION
In 1976, two young men with no money, no office and no company built a computer in a garage. This is the short story of how Apple went from a hand-built circuit board to the Apple II, the iPod, the iPhone, the iPad and a company worth trillions.
